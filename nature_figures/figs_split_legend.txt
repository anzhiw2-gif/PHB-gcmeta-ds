# Split figures — legends (Fig. 1 / 2 / 3)

Source data: gcMeta 2025 species-level representative MAGs carrying PHB-degradation
orthologues (PHA Depolymerase Engineering Database classification mapped onto KEGG:
K05973 phaZ PHB depolymerase; K22249/K22250 phaZ PHO depolymerase; K07518 3HB-dimer
hydrolase; K00019 3-hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase; K01907 acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase;
synthesis reference phaA/phaB/phaC = K00626/K00023/K03821). All counts are exact
annotation-layer retrievals (DIAMOND, e-value <= 1e-5).

## Fig. 1 (fig3a_split_tiers) | How many

**a**, genomes carrying at least one PHB-degradation orthologue, by tier:
Tier 1 depolymerase-positive (K05973/K22249/K22250), n = 8,073; Tier 2 3HB
mobilisation (K07518, or K00019 with K01907) without a depolymerase, n = 1,402;
Tier 3 any remaining combination, n = 8,822 (total 18,297).

**b**, composition of each tier by PHB-synthesis gene content (With phaC: K03821;
Other synthesis: K00626/K00023 without K03821; None).

## Fig. 2 (fig3b_split_ecology) | Where

**a**, species counts per KO summed over the 50 biome catalogues within each of the
12 biome groups (a species occurring in several catalogues of one group is counted
once per catalogue); log10(x + 1) colour scale; white numbers are exact sums; groups
ordered by K05973 count.

**b**, the 10 biome catalogues with most PHB-degradation-genome memberships
(a genome may belong to several catalogues).

**c**, the 10 GTDB phyla with most PHB-degradation genomes.

## Fig. 3 (fig3c_split_genelevel) | Which type

**a**, gene loci retrieved for the depolymerase / hydrolase orthologues
(K05973, K22249, K22250, K07518); log10 y-axis.

**b**, species-level genomes carrying each of the six PHB-degradation KOs; log10 y-axis.

Source data: data/out/phb_degradation_genomes.tsv, phb_degradation_loci.tsv,
phb_deg_distribution_by_catalogue.tsv, catalogue_groups.tsv.

Defects found and fixed during QA (kept for the audit trail)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hero heatmap matrix was not transposed (50 catalogues plotted as rows while
   6 KO tick labels were attached), which compressed all y labels into ~5 pt.
   Fixed by transposing to (6 KOs) x (50 catalogues).
2. Row-heavy panels (15 biomes, 12 phyla) were placed in the shortest grid row;
   their tick labels overlapped by up to 100 %. Fixed by moving them into the
   tallest row (height_ratios 2.9) and adding y-limits padding.
3. Long vertical catalogue labels on the hero panel reached into the panel
   below; fixed with abbreviated display names (<= 11 characters) plus
   every-fourth-column labelling (all 50 columns still plotted).
4. Value labels on log-scale panels touched bar edges or the bottom spine when
   placed inside short bars; replaced by a fixed value strip above the bars.
5. Panel g legend collided with n labels; the redundant n labels were removed
   (totals are shown in panel b).
6. A PowerShell text rewrite corrupted the script encoding; the source was
   rewritten from scratch and re-validated (this file name is authoritative).

Data-integrity notes
--------------------
- No genomes or loci were excluded. Panel a plots all 50 catalogues (tick text
  thinned only); panel d shows the top 15 and panel e the top 12 categories of
  longer rankings.
- Counts are annotation-layer retrievals, not sequence-level re-annotation:
  the limits of KO-based evidence are discussed in docs/03_results.md.
- Rendered panel-by-panel inspection at final size: this environment cannot
  display raster previews, so delivery relies on the four automated audits plus
  the measured PDF geometry recorded in the alignment and collision JSON files.

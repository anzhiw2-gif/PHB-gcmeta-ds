# Fig. 1 — Legend

**Fig. 1 | PHB-degradation potential across gcMeta metagenome-assembled genomes.**

Panels show species-level representative MAGs from gcMeta 2025
(Sun et al., *Nucleic Acids Res* 54:D724-D733; doi:10.1093/nar/gkaf1115) carrying
PHB-degradation orthologues defined by the PHA Depolymerase Engineering Database
classification (Knoll et al., *BMC Bioinformatics* 10:89; doi:10.1186/1471-2105-10-89)
mapped onto KEGG orthologues (K05973 phaZ PHB depolymerase; K22249/K22250 phaZ
PHO depolymerase; K07518 3HB-dimer hydrolase; K00019 3-hydroxybutyrate
dehydrogenase; K01907 acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase; synthesis reference
phaA/phaB/phaC = K00626/K00023/K03821).

**a**, species counts per KO summed across the 50 biome catalogues within each of
the 12 biome groups (a species occurring in several catalogues of one group is
counted once per catalogue); log10(x + 1) colour scale; white numbers are exact
sums.

**b**, genomes carrying at least one PHB-degradation orthologue (n = 18,297),
stratified by tier — Tier 1: depolymerase-positive (K05973/K22249/K22250);
Tier 2: 3HB mobilisation (K07518, or K00019 with K01907) without a depolymerase;
Tier 3: any remaining combination — and by co-occurrence of PHB synthesis genes
(With phaC: K03821; Other synthesis: K00626/K00023 without K03821; None).

**c**, the ten biome catalogues with most PHB-degradation-genome memberships
(a genome may belong to several catalogues).

**d**, the ten GTDB phyla with most PHB-degradation genomes.

**e**, gene loci retrieved for the depolymerase/hydrolase orthologues
(K05973, K22249, K22250, K07518); y-axis is logarithmic.

All data derive from gcMeta annotations (DIAMOND, e-value <= 1e-5) at the
species-representative MAG level; n and tier definitions as in b.
Source data: `data/out/phb_degradation_genomes.tsv`,
`data/out/phb_degradation_loci.tsv`,
`data/out/phb_deg_distribution_by_catalogue.tsv`.
